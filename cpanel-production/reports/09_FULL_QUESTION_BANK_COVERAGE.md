## Status Definitions
- **FULL:** Enough questions for a complete real-feel mock (40+ per section)
- **PARTIAL:** Enough for meaningful practice (15+ questions); full mock needs more
- **SAMPLE ONLY:** Minimal questions (under 15); practice possible but limited
- **SHELL ONLY:** Practice set records exist but no direct questions

## What This Report IS Claiming
- 464 original, exam-aligned practice questions exist in the bank

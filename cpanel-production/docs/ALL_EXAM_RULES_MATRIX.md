## English Proficiency Exams

### 1. IELTS Academic
- **Style label:** IELTS-style practice test
- **Category:** English Proficiency
- **Who:** Students applying to universities in UK, Canada, Australia, etc.
- **Purpose:** Academic English proficiency for university admission
- **Sections:** Listening (30 min), Reading (60 min), Writing Task 1 (20 min), Writing Task 2 (40 min), Speaking Part 1/2/3 (11-14 min)
- **Total duration:** 2h 44m
- **Question types:** MCQ, matching, True/False/Not Given, sentence completion, summary completion, short answer, diagram labeling, writing, speaking
- **Questions per section:** Listening 40, Reading 40, Writing 2, Speaking 3 parts
- **Scoring scale:** Band 0-9
- **Score mapping:** Practice % → Band (90%+ = 8.5, 80% = 7.5, 70% = 7.0, 60% = 6.5, 50% = 6.0)
- **Allowed tools:** None
- **Retake rule:** Unlimited practice; free quota applies (3 ALO / 1 public)
- **Speaking rule:** Recorded via microphone; 3 parts; preparation time for Part 2
- **Writing rule:** Task 1 report (150 words), Task 2 essay (250 words)
- **Listening rule:** Audio plays once; no replay
- **Proctor rule:** Digital proctor logs tab switches, pause/resume, refresh
- **Equipment:** Laptop/desktop, headphones, microphone, stable internet
- **Report format:** Smart Score Report with band estimate, CEFR, skill breakdown
- **Disclaimer:** Practice exam only. Estimated score only. Not official exam result.
- **Last verified:** 2026-01-20
- **Source:** Original exam-aligned practice rules. Not official exam content.
- **Admin editable:** Yes
